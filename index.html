About

I study Operations Research with a Finance specialization and a minor in Artificial Intelligence at West Point. Over the past year I've focused on the mathematical foundations of predictive modeling through machine and reinforcement learning research in the Department of Mathematical Sciences, with hopes of partnering with SOCOM Ignite in the semesters ahead.

I'm drawn to the intersection of machine learning and military operations, especially how autonomous systems, from aerial to undersea, are reshaping the battlefield. I'm a Stokes Writing Fellow and Dean's List honoree, and outside the classroom I've earned the Norwegian Foot March Badge and intend to commission as an Infantry officer upon graduation.
